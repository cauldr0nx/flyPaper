The body is the published NeuroMechFly v2 model (Lobato-Rios et al., Nature Methods 2024; Apache-2.0) — a real Drosophila reconstructed from microscopy. It is staging, not a simulation: nothing about the pose is computed from the circuit, and the model is not spatially registered to the connectome. The text on the monitor is real: those are the log lines the run actually produced. It startles when a response clears the cutoff and is surfaced — that trigger is the run’s own decision, but the movement is invented, because nothing here models a body to compute one from. Read it as a notification. Surfacing is rare (9 times in this run), so use ⇥ next surfaced to catch one.
